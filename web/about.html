Every drive, tracked

Start a trip, or let Convoy notice when you start driving. Afterwards you get the full story.

  • Route colored by speed, with a speed distribution
  • Distance, duration, top speed and replay
  • Monthly timeline and stats, synced across devices

Ride as a convoy

Share a code and everyone appears live on one map with their car and speed, so nobody gets lost and nobody gets left behind.

  • “Fell behind” alerts and arrival notifications
  • Clubs, events and tickets for car meets
  • Online status for friends, leaderboards by distance

Your 3D garage

Add the cars you drive (or dream about), pick a paint, and choose which one friends see moving along your route.

Make the map yours

3D buildings, map themes like Neon City and Blocks, and a heads-up for what's ahead.

Speed limitsThe posted limit on your road, red when you're over.
Camera alertsSpeed and license-plate cameras from OpenStreetMap, worldwide.
Road reportsHazards, closures and police reported by drivers nearby.
