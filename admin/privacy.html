Privacy Policy

The short version:

  • We don’t sell your data and don’t show third-party ads.
  • Live group positions are never stored. They pass through to the people in your drive and disappear.
  • Trips stay on your device unless you’re signed in, in which case they sync to your account.
  • You can delete your account and its data at any time from Profile → Delete account.

2. Location

  • While the app is open, location shows you on the map and powers the speedometer.
  • In the background, location is used only while you’re recording a trip, navigating, sharing with a group drive, or have automatic drive detection turned on. Your phone shows an indicator when this is happening, and you can turn it off in Convoy or your device settings.
  • Group drives: your live position is sent over an encrypted connection to the members of that drive only, while you share. These live positions are not saved on our servers.
  • Friends: if you start sharing with friends, your last-known position is stored so they can see it. It is deleted when you stop sharing.
  • Search and routes: when you search for a place or start navigation, the search text or start and end points are sent to the map services listed in section 4. Map tiles are loaded from OpenFreeMap.

3. How we use information

We use information to provide and secure the Service, sync your data across devices, send notifications you’ve enabled, process purchases, prevent fraud and abuse, respond to you, and fix and improve Convoy. Where laws like the GDPR apply, our legal bases are: performing our contract with you; your consent (location and notifications, which you can withdraw in settings); our legitimate interests in keeping Convoy safe and working; and legal obligations.

We do not sell or rent personal information and do not share it for cross-context behavioral advertising.

5. How long we keep it

  • Account, profile, trips, friends and clubs: until you delete them or your account.
  • Live drive positions: not stored. Drive membership records are kept while your account exists.
  • Friend location: deleted when you stop sharing.
  • Road reports: expire automatically (from 30 minutes to 7 days depending on the type) and are deleted a day after they expire.
  • Purchase records: kept as long as required for tax and accounting laws, even after account deletion.
  • Moderation and security logs: kept as long as needed to keep the Service safe.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Location and notifications: change them any time in Convoy or your device settings.
  • Edit your profile, delete trips, remove friends, leave drives and clubs in the app.
  • Delete your account in Profile → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and associated data, except records we must keep by law. Cancel any App Store or Google Play subscription separately in the store.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, and to object to or restrict certain processing. Contact us using the details below. We won’t discriminate against you for using these rights. You can also complain to your local data protection authority.

7. Security

Data is encrypted in transit, access is limited by row-level security so users can only read what they are allowed to, and payment details are handled by Stripe, Apple or Google rather than by us. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

8. Children

Convoy isn’t intended for anyone under 16, and we don’t knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

We and our providers may process information in the United States and other countries. Where required, we rely on appropriate safeguards such as Standard Contractual Clauses.
